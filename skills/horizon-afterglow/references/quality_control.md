# Quality Control

## Failure Signals

Targeted repair is required when:

1. prose is fluent but the scientific point remains hard to recover;
2. cohesion is under-signaled: a valid relation exists, but adjacent sentences feel unnecessarily disconnected;
3. cohesion is over-signaled: transitions repeatedly state relations that are already obvious or weakly supported;
4. a paragraph is locally polished but its function is unclear;
5. sentence structure becomes more formal but not more readable;
6. redundant defensive framing, disclaimers, or repeated caveats obscure the supported point;
7. repetitive rhythm, decorative symmetry, or boilerplate endings dominate nearby prose;
8. directness or readability improves but claim strength, causality, scope, uncertainty, or a necessary limitation drifts;
9. concision removes an important condition, number, comparison, or qualification;
10. the same result is repeatedly repackaged as a new major finding.

## Final Reader Test

Before delivery, ask:

1. What did this section accomplish?
2. Does each important paragraph have a clear main function?
3. Can the reader find the main action without unnecessary rereading?
4. Does new information have enough context?
5. Do adjacent sentences have enough, but not excessive, connective signaling?
6. Is the supported point stated directly where possible, or buried under redundant defensive framing?
7. Are evidence-required hedges, limitations, and boundary conditions still intact?
8. Is any word or phrase vague, unnatural, unnecessarily elevated, or stronger than the evidence warrants?
9. Is any sentence harder than the underlying idea requires?
10. Is repeated rhythm stylistic or scientifically functional?
11. Do claims match the strength of the evidence?
12. Did any edit change technical meaning, agency, causality, scope, uncertainty, or a necessary limitation?

If 11 or 12 fails, repair is mandatory.

## Repair Strategy

Repair locally, escalating scope as little as possible. The escalation ladder is
defined once in `SKILL.md`, Pass 7; apply it rather than restating it here.
