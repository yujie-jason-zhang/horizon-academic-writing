# Section-Specific Guidance

## Purpose

Different manuscript sections answer different reader questions. Use these as heuristics, not rigid templates.

## Abstract

The reader should recover quickly:

**problem/context → study action → primary result → evidence-proportional conclusion**

Check that the primary result is visible and the conclusion is not broader than the manuscript supports.

## Introduction

A useful progression is often:

**known context → specific limitation/gap → consequence → present study → contributions**

Check whether the contribution actually responds to the stated gap. Do not inflate the gap merely to make the study sound more important.

## Related Work

Prefer conceptual organization by method family, problem setting, theoretical distinction, or limitation relevant to the present paper.

Avoid serial literature narration when it does not help position the present work.

## Methods

Prioritize:

- procedural/dependency order;
- referential clarity;
- module inputs and outputs;
- mechanism relationships;
- reproducibility;
- agreement between prose and mathematical definitions.

Preserve repeated structures when they reflect genuinely parallel operations.

## Experiments / Results

Keep separate:

- comparison/context;
- observation;
- qualification;
- interpretation.

Do not turn a small numerical gain into a substantial improvement, one benchmark into general superiority, or statistical significance into practical significance.

## Discussion

Preserve the boundary among:

**result → interpretation → possible explanation/mechanism → relation to prior work → implication → limitation**

Do not rewrite a plausible explanation as demonstrated causality.

## Conclusion

Recover the solved problem and evidence-supported takeaway without introducing:

- a new contribution;
- a new mechanism;
- a new result;
- a broader claim than the manuscript supports.

## Figures, Tables, and Captions

Keep language direct, reasonably self-contained, consistent with manuscript terminology and values, and limited to interpretations supported by the corresponding evidence.
